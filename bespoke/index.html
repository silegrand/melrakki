Bespoke off-grid power

Off-grid power, engineered to your application.

Our standard platforms cover the common cases. When your requirement does not fit one, that is where Melrakki does its best work: we engineer the autonomous power system around your device, your site and your constraints, with the same discipline and the same proven hardware.

When a standard platform will not do

Most off-grid power problems don't come in a standard size.

The Harka range and Greni are productised for the patterns we see most often. Plenty of requirements sit outside them, and those are the ones worth engineering properly rather than forcing into a catalogue.

01

An unusual load or device

A payload with a heavy peak, an odd voltage, a demanding duty cycle, or a piece of equipment that has to be integrated and powered as one system rather than bolted onto a tower.

02

A specific environment

Somewhere a standard platform was never sized for: a harder winter, a hotter climate, a marine or high-altitude site, or an access window that makes a service visit rare and expensive.

03

A hard constraint

A limit on size, weight, portability, signature, mounting or timeline that the requirement has to be built around, not compromised against.

The same engineering, built to your brief

We don't start from a catalogue. We start from the load.

Bespoke does not mean starting from nothing. It means the same method behind every Melrakki platform, applied to a requirement that happens to be one of a kind. We characterise the exact load, its duty cycle and the conditions it has to survive, then engineer generation, storage, power management, backup and monitoring around it, using proven hardware so nothing critical rests on an unproven part. The approach is set out in full in designing power from the load backwards.

That covers a wide space: solar, battery and hybrid architectures; integration with your own equipment; the communications and remote monitoring the site needs; and the form factor the deployment demands, whether that is a mast, a skid, an enclosure or something built to mount on your asset directly. The same resilience thinking applies as everywhere else we work, described in resilience at the remote edge: reduce the dependencies that can fail, and design for the worst credible week rather than the best day.

Capability and capacity

The capability to engineer it, and the capacity to build it.

Bespoke work is an extension of proven capability, not a leap into the unknown. Melrakki designs and builds its own platform architecture, drawing on more than a decade of deploying off-grid solar surveillance and autonomous power into demanding sectors, from oil and gas to highways, construction and critical infrastructure, including solar CCTV infrastructure deployed across 58 Chevron and Tengizchevroil sites in Kazakhstan, and granted patents in solar-powered surveillance and autonomous off-grid power. When a requirement falls outside the standard range, that same team engineers the answer.
