The problem

The systems that matter most now run beyond the grid.

Cameras, sensors, radios, networks and drones are pushed further from mains power every year, onto sites a connection was never going to reach. Every one of them stops the moment its power does. The usual answer is a diesel generator, and it brings a convoy of its own problems: fuel to haul, emissions to account for, noise and heat that give a position away, maintenance across scattered sites, and fuel worth stealing. Melrakki takes that dependency away.

The approach

Engineered from the load backwards.

We do not sell power off a shelf. We design each platform around the exact load it has to sustain, its power draw, its duty cycle and the conditions it has to survive, then integrate proven power hardware so that nothing critical rests on an unproven part. The result is a system built for one job: keeping your equipment alive, unattended, in the places where a site visit is a helicopter, a convoy or a shutdown.

Melrakki Greni
MK-GR-01
The den

An off-grid charging and docking station for autonomous drones, so persistent and beyond-line-of-sight operations keep flying with no power at the landing zone. Built on skid-mounted solar hybrid power, with a tilting solar array and lithium storage.

Melrakki Harka Hybrid
MK-HK-H6
The endurance tier

Solar, battery and a Stage V backup generator running on HVO renewable diesel, to hold uptime through low light, deep winter and heavy loads. Silent and zero-emission in battery mode; the generator engages only to recharge.

  • Mast6 m galvanised
  • Solar array1080 W (3 x 360 W)
  • Battery24 VDC, 220 Ah
  • Runtimeup to 1840 h at 100 W
  • BackupHVO Stage V generator
